The Problem

Privacy lost when
you need help most.

01
No Privacy in Emergency People must share personal phone numbers to contact vehicle owners, risking privacy and safety.
02
Inefficient Communication No standardized way to handle parking complaints or emergency situations quickly.
03
Lack of Documentation Incidents go undocumented, leading to repeat offenses with no accountability trail.
🔒
The Solution

Four features,
one seamless platform.

Every feature designed to protect privacy while enabling instant, meaningful communication.

📱
QR-Based Contact
Unique QR codes for each vehicle enable contactless, private communication without sharing personal numbers.
🅿️
Parking Complaint
Structured complaint flow with photo evidence and auto-notification to vehicle owners in real-time.
🆘
Emergency Contact
One-tap emergency alerts with location data sent instantly to the vehicle owner and registered contacts.
💳
Vehicle Wallet
Digital vehicle identity storing documents, insurance, and history — accessible via secure QR verification.
