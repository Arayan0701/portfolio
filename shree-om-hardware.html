Problem

The Traditional Workflow Was Broken

Hardware store owners were drowning in paperwork, manual entry errors, and zero visibility into business health.

01

Paper-based inventory

Stock was tracked in notebooks — impossible to search, update, or audit quickly.

02

Manual billing chaos

Each invoice took 10+ minutes to write. Errors were frequent and calculations unreliable.

03

No customer visibility

No way to track who bought what, outstanding credit, or repeat customers.

04

Zero analytics

Business decisions made purely on gut feeling — no data, no reports, no trends.
